The negative $R^2$ values are universal and expected --- a property of financial return predictability, not a modelling failure.

\subsection{Freshness Cutoff Sensitivity}

Table~\ref{tab:freshness} reports how the JKP + FinBERT model's predictive power changes with the maximum age of the earnings call signal.

\midrule

\bottomrule

\end{table}

The freshest sub-sample (calls within 1 month) yields Rank IC of 0.144 --- three times higher than the 3-month cutoff. This confirms that the earnings call signal decays rapidly: the information advantage dissipates within 2--3 months, consistent with the gradual price discovery literature \cite{hong2000}. The 3-month cutoff used in our main analysis represents a practical trade-off between signal strength and sample coverage.

\textbf{Structural finding on JKP.} When evaluated using \textit{monthly} Rank IC (one Spearman correlation per month, cross-sectional), JKP-only yields NaN. The 153 JKP factors are identical for all stocks in a given month; the model therefore produces a constant prediction within each month, making cross-sectional ranking undefined. \textbf{JKP factors are time-series signals (market timing) and cannot perform cross-sectional stock selection by design.}
