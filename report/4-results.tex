\subsection{Feature Engineering Ablation}
\label{sec:results}

Table~\ref{tab:preproc} isolates the contribution of each preprocessing step. Raw FinBERT levels alone are not exploitable (Sharpe $-0.75$). The freshness filter is the most critical step: retaining only calls within 3 months of the prediction date raises Sharpe to 0.28. Delta features incrementally improve Rank IC (0.019 $\to$ 0.026), capturing the quarter-over-quarter surprise component. Cross-sectional z-scoring within each prediction month is the final and most impactful step (Sharpe 0.14 $\to$ 0.79): by ranking each firm relative to its peers in the same month, the model directly targets the cross-sectional objective of the long-short strategy. Ridge regression on the full feature set (Sharpe $-0.10$) performs poorly, suggesting the exploitable component of the signal is not well captured by a simple linear specification.

\subsection{Signal Persistence and Robustness}

\textbf{Post-training IC path (108 months).} Using the model trained on 2008--2014 with early stopping monitored on 2015--2018, we evaluate the full 2015--2023 post-training prediction path. FinBERT-only achieves mean monthly IC 0.0290, positive in 68\% of months, and IC $t$-statistic 3.79. Because 2015--2018 is also the early-stopping window, the formal untouched test period remains 2019--2023; the 108-month path is used as a persistence diagnostic. JKP-only yields undefined monthly IC (within-month constant predictions). Full rolling results are in Appendix~E.

\textbf{Sub-period performance.} FinBERT-only is profitable in all three sub-periods: pre-COVID 2019 ($+3.87\%$, Sharpe 0.50), COVID 2020 ($+14.99\%$, Sharpe 0.77), and post-COVID 2021--2023 ($+12.65\%$, Sharpe 0.87). The strongest sub-period is \textit{after} the COVID-19 shock, suggesting performance is not driven by a single episode. Transaction cost sensitivity ($0.1\%$--$0.4\%$ one-way) is reported in Appendix~D.
